1 Comorbidity burden Assessed per-condition SHAP 0.358 -- dominant predictor. Total count outperforms any single comorbidity. Each additional condition meaningfully increases risk. Strongest and most stable predictor across all model versions.
2 Anemia / Hemoglobin Hgb >=10 g/dL (F) / >=11 g/dL (M) SHAP 0.136 (flag) + 0.073 (value). Anemia flag rose to rank 2 after removing surgery year. Both flag and continuous value are independently predictive. Graded risk across severity levels.
3 Criterion violations (total) COSI composite score SHAP 0.102. Violation count captures cumulative borderline risk that binary eligibility rules miss. Patients with multiple near-threshold values carry risk independent of any single criterion.
4 Age >=45 years SHAP 0.087. Non-linear predictor. Younger patients (<60) carry higher risk due to inflammatory and metabolic disease burden driving early TJA -- a counter-intuitive finding consistent with clinical literature.
5 Creatinine <1.5 mg/dL SHAP 0.084. Non-linear risk acceleration above 1.5 mg/dL. Consistent top predictor across all model versions. Always collected regardless of comorbidity flags.
6 BMI <40 kg/m2   40-44 conditional SHAP 0.064. Risk increases meaningfully at BMI >=35; threshold effect at 40. Higher BMI patients who reach surgery represent a selected, well-optimized group -- model reflects this non-monotone pattern.
7 Systolic BP <160 mmHg SHAP 0.061. Threshold effect above 160 mmHg. Non-linear -- very low systolic BP may reflect frailty or low cardiac output, contributing to elevated risk at both extremes.
8 ASA Class I-II   III case-by-case   IV excluded SHAP 0.058 -- rose to rank 8. Higher independent weight after removing surgery year, suggesting ASA captures genuine physiologic risk signal when temporal confounding is removed.
9 Absolute exclusion count Per COSI criteria SHAP 0.054. Total number of absolute exclusion flags carries independent signal beyond any individual criterion. Patients with multiple exclusions carry compounding risk.
10 Prior ED visits Not in published criteria SHAP 0.052. Strongest utilization predictor. Any prior ED visit carries independent risk beyond comorbidity count. Captures healthcare fragility not reflected in diagnosis codes alone.
11 Prior hospitalizations Not in published criteria SHAP 0.048. Inpatient utilization history independently predicts 90-day adverse events. Works in concert with ED visits to capture prior healthcare burden.
12 Albumin >=3.5 g/dL SHAP 0.046. Important nutritional marker. Borderline 3.0-3.5 g/dL also carries elevated risk. Gated to liver disease patients in the calculator.
13 Recent MI or stroke Absolute exclusion (<6 months) SHAP 0.042. Maintains high independent risk regardless of overall score. Inpatient setting recommended regardless of predicted probability when this flag is active.
14 WBC (pre-op) 4-11 x10^3/uL SHAP 0.042. Abnormal WBC and not-ordered both carry independent risk. Gated to leukopenia patients in the calculator.
-- Procedure (THA vs TKA) Both eligible per guidelines Not in top 15 after outcome refinement. After removing procedure-specific post-operative outcomes (dislocation, revision, periprosthetic fracture), THA and TKA carry comparable pre-operative risk. Cross-validated AUC: THA 0.747, TKA 0.735.
-- Insulin-dependent diabetes Absolute exclusion (most guidelines) Low individual SHAP weight. Most common exclusion criterion among expansion candidates (24.8% of pool). Blanket exclusion may be overly conservative -- model assigns risk based on overall burden, not any single criterion alone.
